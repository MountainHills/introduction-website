How We Operate

One small team, six cafés, one standard.

It's not just the three founders — it's the baristas and café staff who show up every day and hold every cart and every café to the same checklist.

1 / 5

We're a small team by design — baristas and café staff first, with the three founders still showing up to pack the cart right alongside them.

We source beans through respected Philippine roasters, keep a registered corporation and protected brand behind the name, and treat every dealership café and every event cart the same way: same recipe, same setup, same checklist.

That's the part you don't see in the party photos — but it's why hosts and dealership partners keep calling our team back.
